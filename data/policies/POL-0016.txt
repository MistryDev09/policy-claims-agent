POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0016
Policyholder: Anika Govender
Coverage Type: Pet Insurance (Comprehensive)
Insured Animal: One registered canine
Annual Benefit Limit: R40,000
Policy Start Date: 1 November 2023
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Covers veterinary expenses for sudden illness and accidental injury,
including surgeries, hospitalisation, and prescription medication, up to
the annual limit. A sub-limit of R5,000 applies to tick-borne diseases.

WAITING PERIOD
14 days for accidental injury and 60 days for illness. No waiting period
applies to routine annual vaccinations.

EXCLUSIONS
- Hereditary or congenital conditions, specifically hip dysplasia and
  elbow dysplasia
- Dental scaling, polishing, and extraction of deciduous teeth
- Costs related to elective procedures such as sterilisation or
  microchipping

CLAIMS PROCESS
Submit the detailed veterinary invoice and the animal's complete medical
history. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the veterinary
treatment date.
