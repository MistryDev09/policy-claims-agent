POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0014
Policyholder: Lerato Maseko
Coverage Type: Motor Insurance - Third-Party, Fire and Theft
Vehicle: Insured private vehicle
Insured Value: R120,000
Policy Start Date: 15 May 2024
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Covers legal liability for damage caused to third-party vehicles or
property by the insured vehicle, plus loss or damage to the insured
vehicle strictly from fire, lightning, explosion, theft, or attempted
theft. Does NOT cover collision or accident damage to the insured
vehicle under any circumstances.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days for third-party liability and fire damage. A 30-day waiting period
applies to theft and hijacking claims from the Policy Start Date.

EXCLUSIONS
- Any accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle, regardless of
  fault
- Theft occurring while the keys were left in or on the vehicle
- Damage caused to third-party property while driving under the influence
  of alcohol

CLAIMS PROCESS
Submit a completed claim form and a SAPS case number within 48 hours for
theft. For third-party claims, provide the third party's contact details
and vehicle registration.
