COVER DESCRIPTION
Comprehensive cover including accidental damage, fire, theft, hijacking, and
third-party liability up to R5,000,000. Excess of R6,500 applies to all claims
unless the policyholder is under 25, in which case an additional young driver
excess of R5,000 applies.

WAITING PERIOD
Accidental damage and third-party liability are covered from Day 1. A waiting
period of 48 hours applies to theft and hijacking claims from the Policy Start
Date, to prevent immediate-loss fraud.

EXCLUSIONS
- Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Any driver not listed on the policy or not holding a valid licence for the
  vehicle class
- Damage sustained while racing, rallying, or on a closed race track
- Wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown

CLAIMS PROCESS
Accident claims require a police report (CAS number) for any incident involving
theft, hijacking, or third-party injury. Photos of damage and a completed claim
form are required for all claims.
