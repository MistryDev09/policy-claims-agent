POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0015
Policyholder: Hendrik Venter
Coverage Type: Home Insurance (Buildings & Contents)
Buildings Sum Insured: R2,500,000
Contents Sum Insured: R500,000
Policy Start Date: 1 October 2023
Premium Frequency: Annual

COVER DESCRIPTION
Covers the main residential building structure and household contents
against fire, theft, natural disasters, and malicious damage. Total
contents cover is limited to R500,000, with a specific sub-limit of
R20,000 for portable electronic devices and laptops kept in the home.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days for fire, storm damage, and natural disasters. A 7-day waiting
period applies to theft and burglary claims unless forced entry can be
proven.

EXCLUSIONS
- Damage caused by long-term subsidence, heave, or landslip
- Theft occurring if the property is left unoccupied for more than 30
  consecutive days without notifying the insurer
- Damage resulting from rising damp or gradual wear and tear

CLAIMS PROCESS
Provide an inventory of stolen or damaged items, purchase receipts for
electronics, and a SAPS case number for burglary. A loss adjuster
assessment is required for structural damage claims above R50,000.
