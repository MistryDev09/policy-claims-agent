POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0020
Policyholder: Kgomotso Ndlovu
Coverage Type: Funeral Cover (Family Plan)
Main Member Benefit: R50,000
Spouse Benefit: R50,000
Benefit per Child (under 21): R20,000
Policy Start Date: 15 January 2023
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Provides a lump sum cash payout to assist with funeral expenses for the
main member and registered dependents, at the benefit level listed above
for each covered person.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days for accidental death. A 6-month waiting period applies for death
due to natural causes for all members on the policy.

EXCLUSIONS
- Death resulting from participation in war, civil commotion, or riot
- Suicide of any covered member within the first 12 months of being
  added to the policy
- Death of an unregistered dependent not explicitly named on the policy
  schedule

CLAIMS PROCESS
Submit a certified death certificate, the claimant's ID, and a completed
SAPS statement if the death was unnatural. Payouts are typically
processed within 48 hours of complete document submission.
