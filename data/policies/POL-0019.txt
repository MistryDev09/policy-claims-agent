POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0019
Policyholder: Jaco Swanepoel
Coverage Type: Disability Cover (Income Protection)
Monthly Benefit: R35,000
Policy Start Date: 20 August 2021
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Provides monthly income replacement of up to 75% of the policyholder's
gross monthly salary if they become temporarily or permanently unable to
perform their nominated occupation due to illness or injury.

WAITING PERIOD
A 30-day continuous waiting period applies. The policyholder must be
disabled and off work for 30 consecutive days before the first payment
accrues.

EXCLUSIONS
- Disability claims arising from mental health conditions, including
  severe stress, burnout, and depression
- Time off work due to normal, uncomplicated pregnancy or childbirth
- Injuries sustained while participating in professional sports

CLAIMS PROCESS
Provide a detailed report from the treating specialist, a certificate of
booking off work, and three months of payslips prior to the disability
event. Ongoing monthly medical declarations are required.
