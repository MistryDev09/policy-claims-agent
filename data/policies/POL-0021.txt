POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0021
Policyholder: Farzanah Ebrahim
Coverage Type: Travel Insurance (Annual Multi-Trip)
Cover Period: 12 months, trips up to 45 days each
Medical & Evacuation Cover Limit: R5,000,000
Policy Start Date: 5 January 2024
Premium Frequency: Annual

COVER DESCRIPTION
Covers multiple international trips (up to 45 days per trip) over a
12-month period, including emergency medical expenses, medical
evacuation, trip cancellation, and lost baggage. A sub-limit of R10,000
applies to lost electronics or jewellery.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days. Coverage begins immediately upon departure from a South African
international airport, for any trip taken within the 12-month policy
period.

EXCLUSIONS
- Medical expenses resulting from extreme sports, specifically bungee
  jumping, skydiving, and scuba diving below 20 metres
- Travel to countries currently under UN or international trade
  sanctions
- Pre-existing cardiovascular conditions for which the insured received
  treatment in the 6 months prior to travel

CLAIMS PROCESS
For medical emergencies, contact the 24/7 assistance line before
admission. For lost baggage, a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) from
the airline is required within 24 hours of arrival.
