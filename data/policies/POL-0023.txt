POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0023
Policyholder: Naledi Mofokeng
Coverage Type: Device Insurance
Insured Items: Smartphone and smartwatch
Insured Value: R25,000
Policy Start Date: 1 March 2024
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Comprehensive cover for the listed smartphone and smartwatch against
theft, accidental damage, and screen shattering. A maximum of two screen
replacement claims are allowed per 12-month cycle.

WAITING PERIOD
A 14-day waiting period applies to all accidental damage and screen
replacement claims. 0 days for theft claims.

EXCLUSIONS
- Liquid damage or complete submersion in water, regardless of the
  device's IP rating
- Cosmetic damage, such as scratches or dents that do not impact the
  device's functionality
- Loss of the device where no theft is involved (misplacing it)

CLAIMS PROCESS
For theft, a SAPS case number and blacklisting confirmation from the
network provider are required. For screen damage, the device must be
brought to an approved repair centre.
