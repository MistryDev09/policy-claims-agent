COVER DESCRIPTION
This is a limited cover policy. It covers fire damage, theft, hijacking, and
liability to third parties for injury or property damage. It does NOT cover
collision or accidental damage to the policyholder's own vehicle.

WAITING PERIOD
Fire and theft claims are subject to a 48-hour waiting period from the Policy
Start Date. Third-party liability is covered from Day 1.

EXCLUSIONS
- Collision or accidental damage to the insured vehicle (not covered under this
  cover type at all, regardless of fault)
- Use of the vehicle for hire or reward (including e-hailing services such as
  Uber or Bolt) unless the Commercial Use Endorsement has been added (not
  applicable to this policy)
- Damage caused while the vehicle is being driven by an unlicensed driver

CLAIMS PROCESS
Theft and hijacking claims require a police case number. Because this policy
excludes collision cover, claims for own-vehicle collision damage will be
declined outright and the policyholder referred to check whether third-party
liability applies instead.
