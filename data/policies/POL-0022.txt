POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0022
Policyholder: Francois du Toit
Coverage Type: Travel Insurance (Single Trip)
Trip Length: 14 days, Europe
Medical Cover Limit: R2,000,000
Policy Start Date: 10 July 2024
Premium Frequency: Once-off

COVER DESCRIPTION
Comprehensive travel insurance for a single 14-day trip to Europe.
Covers emergency medical costs, personal liability, and trip delays.
Includes a R5,000 sub-limit for cash stolen from the person.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days. Trip cancellation cover begins immediately upon policy purchase;
medical and baggage cover begins on the departure date.

EXCLUSIONS
- Costs associated with mandatory quarantine for a viral outbreak at the
  destination
- Missed flights due to the insured oversleeping or misreading travel
  itineraries
- Theft of baggage left completely unattended in a public space

CLAIMS PROCESS
Submit all original receipts for delayed travel expenses, a police
report for stolen cash, and the original booking invoice.
