POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0017
Policyholder: Xolani Ngcobo
Coverage Type: Life Cover
Sum Assured: R5,000,000
Policy Start Date: 12 March 2022
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Provides a lump sum payment to nominated beneficiaries on the death of
the life assured, intended to cover estate costs, settle debts, and
provide financial security for dependents.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days for death due to accidental causes. A 180-day waiting period
applies for death resulting from natural causes or illness.

EXCLUSIONS
- Death resulting from suicide or intentional self-inflicted injury
  within the first 24 months of the policy
- Death occurring while participating in an illegal act
- Death while acting as a pilot or crew member of an aircraft without a
  valid commercial licence

CLAIMS PROCESS
Beneficiaries must submit a certified copy of the death certificate, the
deceased's ID, and a completed medical attendant's report.
