POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0024
Policyholder: Willem Kruger
Coverage Type: Legal Expenses Cover
Annual Benefit Limit: R150,000
Policy Start Date: 1 September 2022
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Provides legal representation and covers attorney fees for civil,
criminal, and labour disputes. Includes a sub-limit of R10,000 for bail
application assistance.

WAITING PERIOD
A 90-day waiting period applies to all legal disputes. The dispute's
originating event must occur after this waiting period has elapsed.

EXCLUSIONS
- Any legal fees related to divorce, maintenance, or child custody
  disputes
- Pre-existing legal matters or disputes that commenced prior to the
  Policy Start Date
- Defamation suits initiated by the policyholder

CLAIMS PROCESS
Contact the legal helpline to register the dispute before appointing an
attorney. Provide all relevant correspondence, contracts, or notices
related to the legal matter.
