POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0003
Policyholder: Johan van der Merwe
Coverage Type: Life Cover
Sum Assured: R1,200,000
Policy Start Date: 1 June 2023
Premium Frequency: Annual

COVER DESCRIPTION
Full Sum Assured payable to nominated beneficiaries on death of the policyholder from
any cause, subject to exclusions and rider terms below. This policy includes an optional
Hazardous Activities Rider, which the policyholder did NOT purchase at inception.

WAITING PERIOD
Standard 90-day waiting period from Policy Start Date for natural causes of death.
Accidental death is covered from Day 1.

EXCLUSIONS
- Death arising from hazardous hobbies including skydiving, base jumping, or scuba
  diving below 30 metres, unless the Hazardous Activities Rider has been purchased
  (not applicable to this policy)
- Death as a direct result of an act of war or armed insurrection, whether declared
  or undeclared
- Death occurring while the policyholder is a member of a military force on active
  deployment

CLAIMS PROCESS
Beneficiaries submit death certificate, coroner's report where applicable (particularly
important for deaths that may fall under the hazardous activity exclusion), and a
completed claim form.
