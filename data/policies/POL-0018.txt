POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0018
Policyholder: Tariq Desai
Coverage Type: Critical Illness Cover
Sum Assured: R1,000,000
Policy Start Date: 1 February 2024
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Provides a tax-free lump sum on confirmed medical diagnosis of one of the
specified critical illnesses, including cancer, heart attack, stroke, and
organ failure. Payout is tiered based on severity of the illness.

WAITING PERIOD
90 days from the Policy Start Date. Any illness diagnosed within this
90-day window will not be covered, even if the diagnosis is confirmed
after the waiting period has technically expired.

EXCLUSIONS
- Any critical illness directly linked to pre-existing type 2 diabetes
  diagnosed prior to policy inception
- Conditions resulting from intentional self-inflicted injury or
  attempted suicide
- Cancers classified as Stage 0 or in situ

CLAIMS PROCESS
Submit a specialist medical report detailing the exact diagnosis,
histology reports (for cancer), and the completed claim form. The
insurer reserves the right to request an independent medical
examination.
