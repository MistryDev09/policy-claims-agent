POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0013
Policyholder: Sibusiso Zwane
Coverage Type: Motor Insurance - Comprehensive
Vehicle: Insured private vehicle
Insured Value: R350,000
Policy Start Date: 10 January 2024
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Comprehensive coverage for the insured vehicle against accidental damage,
theft, hijacking, fire, and third-party liability. A sub-limit of R5,000
applies to windscreen replacement and R10,000 to emergency roadside
assistance and towing.

WAITING PERIOD
0 days for accidental damage, fire, and third-party claims. A 14-day
waiting period applies to theft and hijacking cover from the Policy Start
Date.

EXCLUSIONS
- Damage occurring while the vehicle is used for commercial fare-paying
  passenger transport (e.g. ride-hailing)
- Accidents caused by the vehicle being driven with unroadworthy tyres
- Claims where the driver does not hold a valid South African driver's
  licence

CLAIMS PROCESS
Submit a SAPS case number within 24 hours for theft or malicious damage.
Provide a completed claim form, driver's licence copy, and photographs of
the accident scene within 30 days of the incident.
