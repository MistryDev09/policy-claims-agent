Coverage Type: Life Cover
Sum Assured: R750,000

COVER DESCRIPTION
Full Sum Assured payable to nominated beneficiaries on death of the policyholder
from any cause, subject to the standard exclusions below. This is a long-standing
policy, well past all waiting periods.

WAITING PERIOD
Standard 90-day waiting period from Policy Start Date, which has long since
elapsed. All causes of death, including natural causes, are fully covered.

EXCLUSIONS
- Death resulting from non-disclosure of a material pre-existing medical
  condition at application stage, discovered post-claim
- Death while committing a criminal act
- Suicide clause no longer applies as the policy is well beyond the 24-month
  exclusion window

CLAIMS PROCESS
Standard claim requirements: certified death certificate, ID copy, and completed
claim form. Given the policy's age, claims are generally processed without
waiting-period disputes.
