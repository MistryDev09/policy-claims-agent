COVER DESCRIPTION
Buildings cover insures the physical structure against fire, storm, and impact
damage up to R1,800,000. Contents cover insures household goods and personal
belongings against fire, theft, and storm damage up to R350,000. These are
separate limits and cannot be combined for a single claim type.

WAITING PERIOD
Fire and storm damage are covered from Day 1. A waiting period of 30 days applies
to theft claims from the Policy Start Date.

EXCLUSIONS
- Gradual deterioration, damp, rot, or wear and tear
- Theft or damage occurring while the property is unoccupied for more than 60
  consecutive days
- Flood damage, where the property is located in a designated flood-risk zone,
  unless the Flood Rider has been purchased (not applicable to this policy)
- Contents claims exceeding the R350,000 contents limit will only be paid up to
  that limit; any excess is not covered

CLAIMS PROCESS
Theft claims require a police report. Buildings and contents claims each require
photographic evidence and, for claims over R50,000, an independent assessor's
report.
