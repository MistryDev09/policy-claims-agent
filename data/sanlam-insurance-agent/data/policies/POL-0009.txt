POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0009
Policyholder: Rajesh Naidoo
Coverage Type: Critical Illness Cover
Sum Assured: R400,000
Policy Start Date: 10 January 2026
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Pays a lump sum of R400,000 on first diagnosis of a listed critical illness,
subject to a 14-day survival period after diagnosis, and subject to the waiting
period and exclusions below.

WAITING PERIOD
A waiting period of 90 days applies from the Policy Start Date for all listed
conditions.

EXCLUSIONS
- Any illness related to HIV/AIDS, unless the HIV Cover Rider has been purchased
  (not applicable to this policy)
- Death or illness resulting from self-inflicted harm
- Conditions for which the policyholder had received medical advice, treatment,
  or diagnosis in the 12 months prior to the Policy Start Date

CLAIMS PROCESS
A specialist diagnosis report and supporting pathology results must accompany
the claim form. Claims filed during the waiting period will be declined and the
policyholder invited to resubmit once the waiting period has passed, provided
the diagnosis date is genuinely after the waiting period ends.
