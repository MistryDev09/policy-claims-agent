POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0001
Policyholder: Thabo Nkosi
Coverage Type: Life Cover
Sum Assured: R500,000
Policy Start Date: 15 January 2024
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
This policy pays out the full Sum Assured of R500,000 to the nominated beneficiary
in the event of the policyholder's death from any cause, subject to the terms below.

WAITING PERIOD
A general waiting period of 90 days applies from the Policy Start Date. Claims arising
from natural causes of death within the first 90 days will not be paid, except in the
case of accidental death, which is covered from Day 1.

SUICIDE CLAUSE
Death by suicide is excluded for the first 24 months of the policy. Thereafter it is
covered under the standard terms.

EXCLUSIONS
- Death caused by self-inflicted injury within the first 24 months of the policy
- Death resulting from non-disclosure of a material pre-existing medical condition
  at application stage
- Death while the policyholder is committing a criminal act

CLAIMS PROCESS
Beneficiaries must submit a certified death certificate, ID copy, and completed claim
form within 90 days of death. Claims are assessed against the exclusions above and the
waiting period rules.
