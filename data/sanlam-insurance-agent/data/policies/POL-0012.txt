POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0012
Policyholder: Chloe van Wyk
Coverage Type: Device Insurance
Insured Item: MacBook Pro 14-inch
Insured Value: R28,000
Policy Start Date: 1 June 2026
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Covers accidental damage and theft of the listed device, up to its insured
value of R28,000, minus a R750 excess per claim.

WAITING PERIOD
A waiting period of 14 days applies to theft claims from the Policy Start Date.
Accidental damage is covered from Day 1.

EXCLUSIONS
- Theft from an unattended device left in a public place (e.g. a car, cafe
  table, or unlocked bag)
- Water damage caused by submersion beyond the device's stated IP rating
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown occurring after the manufacturer's
  warranty period has expired (this is device insurance for accidental damage
  and theft, not an extended warranty)

CLAIMS PROCESS
Theft claims require a police case number. Accidental damage claims require
photos of the damage and a brief written description of how it occurred.
