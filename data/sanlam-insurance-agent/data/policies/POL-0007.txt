POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0007
Policyholder: Sarah Reddy
Coverage Type: Travel Insurance (Single Trip)
Trip Dates: 1 April 2026 - 20 April 2026
Destination: Portugal and Spain
Medical Cover Limit: R100,000
Cancellation Cover Limit: R30,000
Policy Start Date: 1 April 2026 (date of purchase and trip start are the same)

COVER DESCRIPTION
Covers emergency medical expenses up to R100,000, trip cancellation up to
R30,000, and lost baggage up to R15,000, for the duration of the trip dates
listed above only.

WAITING PERIOD
No waiting period applies since this is a single-trip policy purchased for
specific travel dates; cover is active from the trip start date.

EXCLUSIONS
- Any medical condition that existed before the policy was purchased and was
  not declared and accepted in writing by the insurer (pre-existing condition
  exclusion)
- Injury or illness arising from participation in extreme sports (skydiving,
  bungee jumping, mountaineering above 3,000m) unless the Adventure Sports
  Rider was purchased (not applicable to this policy)
- Travel to, or claims arising in, a country under an active government travel
  advisory at the time of travel

CLAIMS PROCESS
Medical claims require original invoices and a medical report. Cancellation
claims require proof of the reason for cancellation (e.g. medical certificate,
death certificate of immediate family).
