POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0008
Policyholder: Zanele Mokoena
Coverage Type: Funeral Cover (Family Plan)
Main Member Benefit: R50,000
Spouse Benefit: R20,000
Benefit per Child: R10,000 (up to 4 children covered)
Policy Start Date: 1 July 2024
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Pays a lump sum on the death of any covered family member, at the benefit level
listed for that family member above, to assist with funeral costs.

WAITING PERIOD
A waiting period of 6 months applies from the Policy Start Date for death from
natural causes. Death by accident is covered from Day 1 with no waiting period.

EXCLUSIONS
- Death by suicide of any covered member within the 6-month waiting period
- Non-disclosed family members added to the policy after a claim event has
  already occurred
- Claims for a family member who was not listed on the policy schedule at the
  time of death

CLAIMS PROCESS
Requires a certified death certificate and completed claim form. Payout is
typically processed within 48 hours of a complete claim submission, given the
urgent nature of funeral costs.
