POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0004
Policyholder: Nomvula Dlamini
Coverage Type: Disability Cover (Income Protection)
Monthly Benefit: R15,000
Annualised Benefit Value: R180,000
Policy Start Date: 1 March 2025
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Pays a monthly income of R15,000 to the policyholder if they become permanently or
temporarily disabled and unable to perform their own occupation, for as long as the
disability persists, up to the policy's maximum benefit period of 24 months per claim.

WAITING PERIOD
A waiting period of 30 days applies from the Policy Start Date. In addition, every
claim has a 7-day deferred period after the date of disability before payments begin.

EXCLUSIONS
- Disability resulting from self-inflicted injury
- Disability arising within the first 12 months of the policy where the underlying
  cause is a pre-existing back or spinal condition present before the Policy Start
  Date
- Disability arising from participation in an illegal activity

CLAIMS PROCESS
Requires a medical practitioner's report confirming inability to perform own
occupation, updated monthly for the duration of the claim.
