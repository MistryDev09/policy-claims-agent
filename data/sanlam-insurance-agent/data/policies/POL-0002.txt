POLICY DOCUMENT
Policy Number: POL-0002
Policyholder: Aisha Patel
Coverage Type: Critical Illness Cover
Sum Assured: R250,000
Policy Start Date: 1 November 2025
Premium Frequency: Monthly

COVER DESCRIPTION
Pays a lump sum of R250,000 on first diagnosis of one of the listed critical illnesses
(cancer, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, major organ transplant), provided the
policyholder survives the applicable survival period of 14 days after diagnosis.

WAITING PERIOD
A waiting period of 180 days applies from the Policy Start Date for all conditions
except accidental trauma-related claims (e.g. loss of limbs due to accident), which
are covered from Day 1.

EXCLUSIONS
- Any cancer diagnosed, or for which symptoms were investigated, before the Policy
  Start Date
- Congenital conditions present from birth
- Illness arising directly from participation in professional contact sport

CLAIMS PROCESS
Claimant must submit specialist medical reports confirming diagnosis, dated after the
waiting period has elapsed. Claims for conditions diagnosed within the 180-day waiting
period will be declined regardless of severity.
